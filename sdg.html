Understand the goal

What does “No Poverty” mean?

SDG 1 calls for ending poverty in all its forms everywhere. Its targets include reducing poverty, strengthening social protection, improving access to resources and basic services, and building resilience to climate, economic and other shocks.

🛡️

Protect people

Social protection, fair access to services, and support during unemployment, illness, disability or disaster can reduce vulnerability.

🌱

Expand opportunity

Skills, decent work, finance and inclusive enterprise can help people build secure livelihoods and participate in economic life.

🌦️

Strengthen resilience

Preparedness, safe infrastructure and climate-aware livelihoods can help households withstand shocks and recover.

Measurement note: The World Bank’s international extreme-poverty line was updated in June 2025 to US$3.00 per person per day in 2021 purchasing-power-parity terms. This is a global comparison measure; it is not Malaysia’s national poverty line.

Interactive classroom activity · 10–15 minutes

Plan a more resilient community

A flood has disrupted income in a low-income Malaysian district. Several households include children, older people and informal workers. Your team has RM100,000 to invest in a one-year response. Choose initiatives, then review the EESG and 3P balance of your plan.

Your task: reduce immediate hardship while helping residents recover and face future shocks. Choose one or more initiatives without exceeding the budget. A strong plan considers who benefits, environmental effects, economic durability and transparent delivery.
Available budget: RM100,000Selected: RM0

Simulation values are illustrative learning scores, not forecasts, official programme costs or poverty statistics.

From game to action

Discuss your choices

  1. Which groups might still be left out of your plan? How would you include their voices?
  2. How does your plan connect People, Planet and Prosperity with EESG?
  3. What evidence would you collect after one year to assess whether poverty and vulnerability have reduced?
  4. How can the programme continue fairly and transparently after the initial budget ends?

Suggested classroom output: present a one-page action plan with your budget, intended beneficiaries, EESG and 3P links, two indicators, and one risk-mitigation measure.
